    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>Hearing these words of Rama, Sita greatly sorry, with tears in her eyes, spoke gently unto him the following words. "The evils, thus enumerated by thee of living in the forest, do thou know, appear as so many good qualities unto me, who have been made forward by thy affection.</p>
