  <h2 class="sanskrit-heading">Ayodhyā Kāṇḍa Sarga 71</h2>
  <h3 class="thematic-heading">Events of Section LXXI</h3>

  <div class="synopsis-box">
    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>Issuing from the palace, the blazing Bharata endowed with prowess went in an easterly direction, and seeing before him the river called Sudama, crossed it. Then the auspicious descendant of Ikshwaku crossed the broad Hradini coursing westwards, as also the river Satadru.</p>
