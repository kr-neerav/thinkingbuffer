  <div class="synopsis-box">
    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>As that best of ladies, Kaucalya, was thus lamenting, Sumitra ever abiding in virtue, spake unto her these words consistent with righteousness,--"O worshipful one, your son is crowned with all qualities,--and is the best of men. Why then do you bewail thus, or weep bitterly?</p>
