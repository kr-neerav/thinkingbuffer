    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>HEARING Rama's words, and seeing him dressed like an ascetic, the king in the midst of his wives was deprived of his senses. And burning in grief, the king could not eye Raghava, nor seeing him could that one of afflicted mind answer anything.</p>
