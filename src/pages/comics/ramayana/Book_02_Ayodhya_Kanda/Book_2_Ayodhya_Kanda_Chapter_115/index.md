  <div class="synopsis-box">
    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>Having placed his mothers in Ayodhya, Bharata steady in his vow, kindling in grief, said unto his preceptors, "I shall go to Nandigrama, and therefore greet you all. There I shall suffer all this grief on account of Rama's absence.</p>
