  <div class="synopsis-box">
    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>Having despatched those monkeys in a southern direction, Sugriva spoke unto the monkey named Sushena, resembling a mass of clouds. Approaching his father-in-law, Tara's father, possessed of dreadful prowess, the king, bowing and with joined hands, spoke unto him.</p>
