    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>Having heard all this related to them by the vulture-king, the monkeys possessing the strength of lions, filled with delight, bounded and set up a roar. Hearing from Sampati that Ravana was to be slain, the monkeys growing glad, came to the ocean, with the desire of seeing Sita.</p>
