  <h2 class="sanskrit-heading">Paraśurāma Garvabhaṅga</h2>
  <h3 class="thematic-heading">Rama strings Vishnu's bow and shoots an arrow destroying Parashurama's celestial realms</h3>

  <div class="synopsis-box">
    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>"O Rama, son of Dacaratha, I have, O hero, heard of thy wonderful prowess; and I have also heard all about thy breaking of the bow. And having heard of that wonderful and inconceivable breaking of the bow, I have bent my steps hither, taking another auspicious bow.</p>
