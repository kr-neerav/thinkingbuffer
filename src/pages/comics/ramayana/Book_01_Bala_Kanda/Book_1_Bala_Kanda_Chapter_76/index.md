  <h3 class="thematic-heading">Dasharatha and the four newlyweds return triumphantly to ecstatic celebrations in Ayodhya</h3>

  <div class="synopsis-box">
    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>Hearing Jamadagni's words, the son of Dacaratha, in consideration of the presence of his father, said these words in subdued tone,--"O Bhrigu's son, I have heard of the deeds thou hast performed, resolved on avenging thy sire. O Brahmana, I acknowledge that.</p>
