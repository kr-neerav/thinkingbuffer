  <div class="synopsis-box">
    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>Having said those words unto Rama, composed of melodious letters, Kaucika again spoke unto Kakutstha, saying, --"Formerly there was a king--lord of Ayodhya named Sagara. And it came to pass that righteous one, though eagerly wishing for children, was without issue.</p>
