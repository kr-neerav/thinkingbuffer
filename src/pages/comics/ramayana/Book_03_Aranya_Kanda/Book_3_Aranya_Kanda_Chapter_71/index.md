    <h4>Original Narrative Summary (Dutt 1891 Prose Translation)</h4>
    <p>O mighty armed Rama of great prowess, formerly my beauty, beyond conception, was known all over the three worlds, like unto the beauty of the Sun, the Moon and Indra. I used to frighten everywhere the ascetics living in the forest by turning this my beauty into a terrific form.</p>
